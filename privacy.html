Privacy

Your details, handled simply

Last updated 16 November 2026.

What the inquiry form collects

When you send an inquiry through the "Plan my event" form, we receive only what you type or choose:

  • your name, phone / WhatsApp number and (if you give it) email address;
  • the celebrations you're planning, the date (or that it's flexible), an approximate guest count;
  • the services you're interested in, a budget range if you choose one, how you'd like us to contact you, and any notes you add;
  • a few technical details to help us read the inquiry: the page it was sent from and your browser type.

Where it is stored

Inquiries are saved in our database hosted by Supabase (a cloud database service). Only the Dreamz Planners owners can sign in and read them. If your connection drops while sending, the inquiry is kept in your own browser and sent the next time you visit the site.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply about your celebration — usually by phone call or WhatsApp. We don't sell or share your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

If you tap "Send on WhatsApp", your inquiry summary opens in WhatsApp and is sent through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores a couple of small preferences (for example, that you closed an announcement) on your own device. Fonts and animation libraries are loaded from public providers (Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr), which receive the usual technical request details such as your IP address.

Your choices
